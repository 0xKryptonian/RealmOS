#### Our Advantages Over Them
1. **More Polished Games**: Chess, Sudoku, Tetris vs their simpler games

3. **Better Hedera Integration**: HTS + HCS + NFTs
4. **AI Agents**: 3 specialized agents vs their 1
5. **Professional UI**: Modern design with shadcn/ui

#### What We Can Learn
- ✅ AI game generation is a killer feature
- ✅ No-code platform attracts creators
- ✅ Focus on innovation wins judges

**Add AI game generation** while maintaining our superior ecosystem and game quality.
